Last Lunar New Year, my family had a reunion at my grandmother's house. My grandmother prepared a feast for it, but I didn't eat that much because I was on a diet. Thus, my grandparents blamed me for wasting food. I have told them that I was keeping in shape and they didn't understand why young people care about their appearance so much, forcing me to finish rest food. From my perspective, I didn't understand the reason that my grandmother cooked so many obviously we couldn't finish all. As a result, we didn't talk anymore after the dinner.

As time goes by, there is growing concern about how to reduce generation gap. In my opinion, I would argue that communicating more is the most useful way. First, when the conflict occur between elders and youth, it is crucial for both sides to express their own thought. Otherwise, we will never break down barriers. Second, we should put ourselves in their shoes. It is a well-known fact that people live in different generation and grown-up environment will have great difference of notions and habits. Hence, only if we see things through their eyes can we avoid further misunderstanding. All in all, I considerate that it is possible for young people and seniors to get along with each other, as long as we put what I said above into practice.
