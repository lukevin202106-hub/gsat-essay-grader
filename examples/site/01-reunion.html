團圓飯

估分14／20

估 14 分。照大考中心 113、114 年寫的五等級，14 分在「可」（10～14 分）的最上面，離「優」（15 分起）差 1 分。題目要的兩件事都寫到了，兩段的分工和順序也清楚；分數主要掉在文法，大錯有 14 處，其中 4 處擠在第二段同一句。全篇 228 字，超過 120 字，分兩段，沒有另外扣分。

範圍約 13～15。輕修版約 16～17，高分版約 18～19。

  • 內容4／5
    做得好
    第一段切題，細節具體：你在節食所以吃得少、長輩說你浪費、你不懂為什麼要煮這麼多、飯後彼此不講話。第二段有主題句（多溝通），也給了兩個做法。
    扣在哪
    第二段的理由都是一般的說法（不說出來就打不破隔閡、不同世代想法不同），沒有例子，也沒有回到第一段那頓飯。第一段停在「不講話」（第 13 處），沒寫你當時或後來怎麼想。照評分表，這是「相關細節」還不夠完整，所以給 4 不給 5。
    怎麼拿滿
    第二段每個做法配一個做得到的例子，例如下次先跟奶奶說自己在控制飲食，請她少煮兩道。結尾回頭提一次那頓團圓飯。第一段最後加一句心情。
  • 組織4／5
    做得好
    分兩段，第一段說經過，第二段說看法，各做各的事。第一段照時間順序寫。第二段有 First、Second、All in all，開頭、發展、結尾都有。
    扣在哪
    轉承語有幾處不合：Thus 放在敘事裡偏硬（第 3 處），and 的地方意思是相反的（第 7 處），As time goes by 接不上第一段（第 14 處），In my opinion 和 I would argue 重複（第 17 處）。另外主題句說的是「多溝通」，Second 寫的是換位思考，跟主題句對不太上；Second 那句和 Hence 那句說的又是同一件事。
    怎麼拿滿
    轉承語照意思選，每句只留一個。主題句改成能包住兩個做法的說法，例如 both sides need to talk and listen；第二點補一句新的內容，不要只換個說法再講一次。
  • 文法句構3／5
    做得好
    第一段幾乎都用過去式，第二段用現在式，分得清楚。句型有變化：句尾的分詞 forcing me to …、It is crucial for … to …（第 22 處）、only if … can we … 的倒裝（第 31 處），這三句都寫對了。
    扣在哪
    大錯 14 處，文法這一項佔 12 處。句子結構：第 11 處兩個子句中間沒有連接詞，第 26 處一句有兩個動詞。時態：第 5 處。主詞動詞一致：第 20 處。名詞單複數和冠詞：第 10、16、19、23、27、29 處。另有第 8 處 rest food、第 12 處 finish all。第 26～29 處都在同一句，那一句讀的人要猜意思。其他的錯不太影響理解，所以落在「可」的 3 分。
    怎麼拿滿
    寫完檢查四件事：敘事有沒有跳出過去式；單數主詞的現在式動詞有沒有加 s；可數名詞前面有沒有冠詞或複數；一個句子是不是只有一個主要動詞。這四件做到，大錯會少掉一大半。
  • 字彙拼字3／5
    做得好
    全篇沒有拼錯字，大小寫也都對。reunion、feast、crucial、perspective、barrier 這幾個精選字用對了，blame … for（第 4 處）、break down barriers（第 24 處）的搭配也正確。
    扣在哪
    用字不當有幾處：grown-up environment 是照中文直翻（第 28 處），considerate 拿來當動詞（第 32 處），notions 的意思偏了（第 30 處）。搭配可以更準的：reduce 配 gap（第 15 處）、useful way（第 18 處）、youth（第 21 處）、keeping in shape（第 6 處）。建議用字八個只用對 reunion 一個。
    怎麼拿滿
    背單字時連詞性和常配的字一起記，例如 consider（動詞）和 considerate（形容詞）、narrow the gap。下次把建議用字再多用上兩三個，像 absurd、the generation gap、Only when …。

題目

Last Lunar New Year, my family had a reunion at my grandmother's house. My grandmother prepared a feast for it⁠1, but I didn't eat that much⁠2 because I was on a diet. Thus,⁠3 my grandparents blamed me for wasting food⁠4. I have told⁠5 them that I was keeping in shape⁠6 and⁠7 they didn't understand why young people care about their appearance so much, forcing me to finish rest food⁠8. From my perspective, I didn't understand the reason that⁠9 my grandmother cooked so many⁠10 obviously we couldn't⁠11 finish all⁠12. As a result, we didn't talk anymore after the dinner⁠13.

As time goes by,⁠14 there is growing concern about how to reduce⁠15 generation gap⁠16. In my opinion, I would argue that⁠17 communicating more is the most useful way⁠18. First, when the conflict⁠19 occur⁠20 between elders and youth⁠21, it is crucial for both sides to⁠22 express their own thought⁠23. Otherwise, we will never break down barriers⁠24. Second, we should put ourselves in their shoes⁠25. It is a well-known fact that people live in⁠26 different generation⁠27 and grown-up environment⁠28 will have great difference of⁠29 notions⁠30 and habits. Hence, only if we see things through their eyes can we avoid⁠31 further misunderstanding. All in all, I considerate⁠32 that it is possible for young people and seniors to get along with each other, as long as we put what I said above⁠33 into practice.

逐句挑錯

33 處
  1. 1小改代名詞

    原句prepared a feast for it

    改成prepared a feast for us

    it 指的是 reunion，讀的人要停一下才看得出來。寫 for us，直接說是煮給誰吃的。

  2. 2小改口語

    原句I didn't eat that much

    改成I didn't eat much

    that much 是講話時的說法，前面也沒有可以比的量。作文裡寫 much 就夠了。

  3. 3小改轉折詞

    原句Thus, my grandparents blamed me

    改成So my grandparents blamed me

    Thus 多用在說理的文章。這一段在講一件事的經過，用 So 比較順。

  4. 4加分片語

    原句blamed me for wasting food

    可以My mother blamed me for coming home late.

    blame 人 for V-ing，介系詞和動名詞都對。blame 是精選字，這個用法留著。

  5. 5大錯時態

    原句I have told them that

    改成I told them that

    這是去年過年的事，前後都是過去式，只有這裡跳成現在完成式。改回過去式 told。

  6. 6小改搭配

    原句I was keeping in shape

    改成I was trying to keep in shape

    keep in shape 說的是長期維持的狀態，不太用進行式。加 trying to，意思是「我想維持身材」，比較接近你要說的。

  7. 7小改轉折詞

    原句keeping in shape and they didn't understand

    改成keep in shape, but they didn't understand

    你解釋了，他們還是不懂，前後的意思相反。and 換成 but，前面加逗號。

  8. 8大錯詞性

    原句finish rest food

    改成finish the rest of the food

    rest 是名詞，不能直接放在 food 前面當形容詞用。「剩下的…」寫 the rest of the ＋名詞。

  9. 9小改冗詞

    原句From my perspective, I didn't understand the reason that my grandmother cooked

    改成From my perspective, it was absurd that my grandmother cooked

    From my perspective 已經是「在我看來」，後面再接 I didn't understand，同一個意思說了兩次。這裡換成 it was absurd that，順便用到建議字 absurd。只想小改的話，拿掉 From my perspective，寫 I didn't understand why 也可以。

  10. 10大錯可數名詞

    原句cooked so many

    改成cooked so many dishes

    many 後面要有可數名詞，這裡沒有。補上 dishes；要說食物的話寫 so much food。

  11. 11大錯句構

    原句my grandmother cooked so many obviously we couldn't finish

    改成my grandmother cooked so many dishes when we obviously couldn't finish

    my grandmother cooked … 和 we couldn't finish … 是兩個句子，中間沒有連接詞。加 when（明明…卻），obviously 移到 we 的後面。

  12. 12大錯代名詞

    原句we couldn't finish all

    改成we couldn't finish them all

    all 很少單獨當受詞，要說出是「全部的什麼」。前面改成 dishes，這裡就寫 them all；前面如果是 food，寫 it all。

  13. 13小改指代

    原句we didn't talk anymore after the dinner

    改成my grandparents and I didn't talk to each other for the rest of the evening

    上一句的 we 是全家人，這一句的 we 是你和祖父母，讀的人要猜。把人寫出來。後面可以再加一句當時的心情，這一段就有收尾。

  14. 14小改邏輯

    原句As time goes by, there is growing concern about

    改成Conflicts like this are common, and there is growing concern about

    As time goes by 是「隨著時間過去」，跟第一段那頓飯接不上。換成一句指回第一段的話，兩段就連起來了。後面的 there is growing concern about 用對了，留著。

  15. 15小改搭配

    原句reduce generation gap

    改成narrow the generation gap

    gap 是「距離」，常配 narrow（縮小）或 bridge（跨過）。reduce 多用在數量上。

  16. 16大錯冠詞

    原句generation gap

    改成the generation gap

    gap 是可數名詞，前面不能空著。「代溝」固定寫 the generation gap，建議用字給的就是這個寫法。

  17. 17小改冗詞

    原句In my opinion, I would argue that

    改成I would argue that

    In my opinion 和 I would argue 都是「我認為」，留一個就好。

  18. 18小改搭配

    原句is the most useful way

    改成is the most effective way to do so

    說方法「有效」用 effective（精選字）。後面加 to do so，指回上一句的縮小代溝，讀的人才知道是做什麼的方法。

  19. 19大錯冠詞

    原句when the conflict occur

    改成when a conflict occurs

    the conflict 是指某一次特定的衝突。這裡講的是任何一次，用 a。

  20. 20大錯主詞動詞一致

    原句the conflict occur

    改成a conflict occurs

    主詞 conflict 是單數，現在式動詞要加 s。

  21. 21小改用字

    原句between elders and youth

    改成between the elderly and the young

    youth 當「年輕人」要寫成 the youth 或 young people。這裡改成 the elderly and the young，兩邊對稱，elderly 是精選字。結尾你寫的是 young people and seniors，全篇用同一組說法會比較整齊。

  22. 22加分句型

    原句it is crucial for both sides to express

    可以It is crucial for parents to listen to their children.

    It is ＋形容詞＋ for 人＋ to V 寫對了，crucial 是精選字。這個句型留著。

  23. 23大錯單複數

    原句express their own thought

    改成express their own thoughts

    thought 當「想法」是可數名詞。兩邊的人各有想法，用複數。

  24. 24加分片語

    原句break down barriers

    可以break down the barriers between us

    break down barriers（打破隔閡）搭配正確，barrier 是精選字。加上 between us，會更清楚是誰和誰之間。

  25. 25小改指代

    原句put ourselves in their shoes

    改成put ourselves in each other's shoes

    their 指誰沒有說。前面的 we 是兩邊的人，所以寫 each other's。下一句 through their eyes 也可以一起改成 through each other's eyes。put oneself in someone's shoes 這個片語本身用對了。

  26. 26大錯句構

    原句people live in different generation … will have

    改成people who grow up in different generations … have

    一句裡有 live 和 will have 兩個動詞，中間沒有連接。加 who，前半就變成形容 people 的子句。live 換成 grow up，「在不同世代長大」，後面的環境也能一起接。

  27. 27大錯單複數

    原句different generation

    改成different generations

    different 後面接可數名詞，要用複數。

  28. 28大錯中式英文

    原句different generation and grown-up environment

    改成grow up in different generations and environments

    grown-up 是「成年人、成年的」，grown-up environment 會被讀成「大人的環境」。「成長環境」是 the environment one grows up in；前面已經改成 grow up in，這裡留 environments 就好。

  29. 29大錯單複數

    原句will have great difference of notions and habits

    改成have great differences in values and habits

    difference 是可數名詞，這裡不只一種差別，用複數；「在…方面的差別」介系詞是 in。講一般的事實用現在式 have，不用 will。

  30. 30小改用字

    原句notions and habits

    改成values and habits

    notion 是「概念、想法」，偏抽象。題目說的「想法」是看事情的觀念，用 values（價值觀，精選字）比較貼近，寫 ideas 也可以。

  31. 31加分句型

    原句only if we see things through their eyes can we avoid

    可以Only when we listen to each other can we narrow the generation gap.

    only if 放在句首，後面 can we 倒裝，寫對了。建議用字的 Only when … 是同一種倒裝，下次可以換著用。see things through someone's eyes 也用得好。

  32. 32大錯詞性

    原句I considerate that

    改成I believe that

    considerate 是形容詞，意思是「體貼的」，不能當動詞。動詞是 consider；這裡說自己的看法，用 believe 比較自然。

  33. 33小改指代

    原句put what I said above into practice

    改成put these two ideas into practice

    what I said above 是「我上面說的」，偏口語，主詞也從 we 跳到 I。上面說了兩個做法，寫 these two ideas 就清楚。put … into practice 用對了。

左邊輕修版：照你原來的句子，把文法和搭配改對，八個建議用字都放進去。右邊高分版：同一頓團圓飯。第一段加了你發脾氣的那一刻和飯後的心情，第二段每個做法配一個例子，結尾回到明年的團圓飯。

輕修版

260 字・約 16～17 分
綠底＝改過的地方，金底＝建議用到

Last Lunar New Year, my family had a reunion at my grandmother's house. My grandmother prepared a feast for us, but I didn't eat much because I was on a diet. So my grandparents blamed me for wasting food. I told them that I was trying to keep in shape, but they didn't understand why young people care about their appearance so much, forcing me to finish the rest of the food. From my perspective, it was absurd that my grandmother cooked so many dishes when we obviously couldn't finish them all. As a result, my grandparents and I didn't talk to each other for the rest of the evening.

Conflicts like this are common, and there is growing concern about how to narrow the generation gap. I would argue that communicating more is the most effective way to do so. First, when a conflict occurs between the elderly and the young, it is crucial for both sides to express their own thoughts without offending each other. Otherwise, we will only pretend that nothing is wrong and never break down barriers. Second, it is worth putting ourselves in each other's shoes. It is a well-known fact that people who grow up in different generations and environments have great differences in values and habits. Hence, only when we see things through each other's eyes can we avoid bias and further misunderstanding. All in all, I believe that it is possible for young people and seniors to get along with each other, as long as we put these two ideas into practice.

句子的順序和內容都照你的，改的是挑錯裡標出來的地方。有兩處換得比較多：第二段開頭照第 14 處換成 Conflicts like this are common，第一段最後一句照第 13 處寫出是誰、到什麼時候。為了放建議用字，加了 without offending each other、only pretend that nothing is wrong、bias and 這幾個字，也把 we should put 換成 it is worth putting、only if 換成 only when。

高分版

261 字・約 18～19 分
底線＝精選字，紫底＝精選片語，紫字＝句型

Last Lunar New Year, my family gathered at my grandmother's house for a reunion dinner. She had prepared a feast for us, but I ate very little because I was on a diet. Seeing my bowl half full, my grandparents blamed me for wasting food. I explained that I was trying to keep in shape, yet they could not understand why young people care so much about their appearance. From my perspective, it was absurd to cook so many dishes that nobody could finish them. When my grandfather insisted that I finish the rest of the food, I lost my temper and called their ideas outdated. My words offended them, and an uneasy silence fell over the table. After that, I pretended to be busy with my phone, though deep down I felt guilty.

Conflicts like this are common, but the generation gap can be narrowed as long as both sides are willing to talk and listen. First, it is crucial for both sides to express their thoughts honestly. If I had told my grandmother about my diet beforehand, she would have cooked less. Second, we should put ourselves in each other's shoes. My grandparents grew up when food was scarce, so to them a full table is a way of showing love. It is worth listening to their stories without bias. Only when we talk openly and listen patiently can we break down the barriers between us. Next Lunar New Year, I will put both ideas into practice so that our reunion dinner can end in laughter instead of silence.

故事和兩個做法都照你的。第一段多了衝突最僵的那一刻，最後補一句心情；第二段兩個做法各配一個例子，最後一句回到團圓飯。有幾處情節是我加的：碗裡還剩一半、爺爺堅持要你吃完、你發脾氣說他們的想法過時、之後假裝在滑手機、心裡過意不去、事先跟奶奶說在節食、爺爺奶奶小時候食物很少、明年過年的打算。這些是示範，請換成你自己的。

    在世界上乾燥的地區，淡水很稀少。
  • biasn.★ L2
    偏見、偏心
    A good judge should not show bias toward either team.
    好的裁判不應該偏袒任何一隊。
  • laughtern.★ L2
    笑聲
    Laughter filled the classroom when the teacher told a funny story.
    老師講了一個好笑的故事，教室裡充滿了笑聲。
精選片語13 條
  • blame sb. for sth.★
    為某事責怪某人
    It is not wise to blame others for your own mistakes.
    因為自己犯的錯而責怪別人並不明智。
  • keep in shape課本片語
    保持身材；維持健康
  • from my perspective★
    就我的觀點來看
    From my perspective, a short walk after dinner does more good than watching television all evening.
    就我的觀點來看，晚餐後散個步，比整晚看電視更有益處。
  • the rest of★
    其餘的；剩下的
    He drank half of the juice and gave the rest of it to his sister.
    他喝了一半的果汁，把剩下的給了妹妹。
  • lose one's temper★
    發脾氣；動怒
    The coach never loses his temper, even when the players make mistakes.
    即使球員犯錯，教練也從不發脾氣。
  • the generation gap★
    代溝
    Talking openly is the best way to reduce the generation gap at home.
    坦誠溝通是縮小家中代溝的最好方法。
  • as long as★
    只要；只要……就
    Students can improve their grades as long as they study a little every day.
    只要每天讀一點書，學生就能提升成績。
  • be willing to V★
    願意
    Many senior students are willing to tutor younger kids for free.
    許多高年級學生願意免費幫學弟妹補習。
  • put oneself in sb.'s shoes★
    將心比心；設身處地
    Before you blame him, try to put yourself in his shoes for a minute.
    在你責怪他之前，先將心比心，設身處地替他想一想。
  • grow up★
    長大；成長
    Children grow up quickly, so enjoy every moment with them.
    孩子長得很快，所以要珍惜和他們相處的每一刻。
  • It is worth V-ing★
    值得…
    It is worth visiting the old market at least once during your stay.
    你停留期間，值得去逛逛那個老市場，至少去一次。
  • Only when …★
    只有當…才…
    Only when we lose something do we realize how valuable it was.
    只有在失去某樣東西時，我們才明白它有多珍貴。
  • break down barriers★
    打破隔閡；消除障礙
    Learning a second language can help break down barriers between people from different cultures.
    學第二外語有助於打破不同文化的人之間的隔閡。
  • instead of★
    而不是；代替
    Why not walk to school instead of taking the bus every morning?
    每天早上何不走路上學，而不是搭公車呢？

句型

高分版用到的 9 個

  1. 分詞構句

    V-ing …, S + V

    Seeing my bowl half full, my grandparents blamed me for wasting food.

    「看到」和「責怪」都是 my grandparents 做的，前一個動作改成 V-ing 放句首。你原文句尾的 forcing me to … 是同一種寫法，寫對了。要留意逗號後面的主詞，得是做 V-ing 那個動作的人。

  2. 間接問句

    S + V + why / how / what + S + V

    I explained that I was trying to keep in shape, yet they could not understand why young people care so much about their appearance.

    why 後面照直述句的順序寫（young people care），不寫 why do young people care。這一段你原文就寫對了。前半句改了三個地方：have told 換成過去式的 explained（第 5 處），keeping 改成 trying to keep（第 6 處），and 換成 yet（第 7 處）。

  3. so … that

    so + many / much + 名詞 + that + 子句

    From my perspective, it was absurd to cook so many dishes that nobody could finish them.

    你原文寫 cooked so many obviously we couldn't finish all：many 後面少了名詞（第 10 處），兩個句子中間沒有連接詞（第 11 處），all 前面少了 them（第 12 處）。用 so many dishes that … 一句就接起來了。

  4. insist that

    insist + that + S + (should) + 原形動詞

    When my grandfather insisted that I finish the rest of the food, I lost my temper and called their ideas outdated.

    insist 後面的子句省略了 should，動詞用原形，所以是 I finish，雖然整句在講過去的事，也不寫 finished。the rest of the food 是第 8 處的改法。

  5. It is … for sb. to V

    It is + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to + 原形動詞

    First, it is crucial for both sides to express their thoughts honestly.

    這一句你原文就寫對了（第 22 處），照著留。只有 thought 改成複數 thoughts（第 23 處）。

  6. 與過去事實相反的假設

    If + S + had + 過去分詞, S + would have + 過去分詞

    If I had told my grandmother about my diet beforehand, she would have cooked less.

    講一件當時沒做的事。if 那一邊用 had told，另一邊用 would have cooked，兩邊不要對調。第二段的做法要配例子的時候，回頭拿第一段的事來假設，就可以用這一句。

  7. It is worth V-ing

    It is worth + V-ing

    It is worth listening to their stories without bias.

    worth 後面接 V-ing，不接 to V，常見的錯是 It is worth to listen。這是建議用字，你原文沒用到。

  8. Only when 放句首的倒裝

    Only when + S + V + 助動詞 + S + 原形動詞

    Only when we talk openly and listen patiently can we break down the barriers between us.

    你原文的 only if we see … can we avoid（第 31 處）倒裝寫對了，這裡換成建議用字 Only when。倒裝的是後半句（can we），when 那半句照平常的順序。break down the barriers 也是你原文的（第 24 處）。

  9. so that（目的）

    S + V + so that + S + can / will + 原形動詞

    Next Lunar New Year, I will put both ideas into practice so that our reunion dinner can end in laughter instead of silence.

    so that 連在一起，後面說目的，動詞前面通常有 can 或 will。跟上面分開寫的 so … that（說結果）不一樣。put … into practice 是你原文結尾的片語，what I said above 換成 both ideas（第 33 處）。

可以直接套用的句子

底線處換成自己的內容。

  • 第一段開頭：交代時間、地點、場合

    Last ___, my family gathered at ___ for a ___.

    時間可以換 Mid-Autumn Festival、summer；地點換 my uncle's house、a restaurant；場合換 reunion dinner、birthday party（前面已經有 a，不要再寫一次）。

  • 第一段：說自己當時的看法

    From my perspective, it was absurd to ___.

    空格放原形動詞開頭的一段，例如 wake up at five on a holiday。absurd 可以換 unfair、unreasonable。

  • 第一段：衝突最僵的那一刻

    When ___ insisted that I ___, I lost my temper and ___.

    insisted that I 後面的動詞用原形（give up、go home）。最後一格寫你說了什麼或做了什麼，用過去式。

  • 第一段結尾：事後的心情

    After that, I pretended to ___, though deep down I felt ___.

    pretended to 後面接原形（be asleep、be busy）；「假裝不在乎」是 pretended not to care。心情可以換 guilty、sorry、hurt。

  • 第二段開頭：接回第一段，帶出主張

    Conflicts like this are common, but ___ can be ___ as long as both sides are willing to ___.

    例如 the generation gap can be narrowed、misunderstandings can be avoided。最後一格放原形動詞，最好能包住後面要講的兩個做法。

  • 第二段：給做法配一個例子

    If I had ___ beforehand, ___ would have ___.

    had 和 would have 後面都接過去分詞（had asked、would have understood）。寫第一段裡當時沒做的那件事。

  • 第二段：收攏兩個做法

    Only when we ___ can we ___.

    前一格放現在式動詞（listen to each other），後一格放原形（narrow the generation gap、avoid misunderstandings）。can we 的順序不要寫回 we can。

  • 結尾：回到第一段的場景

    Next ___, I will ___ so that our ___ can end in laughter instead of ___.

    第一格換成第一段的時間，第三格換成第一段的場合。最後一格放第一段結束時的樣子，例如 silence、tears、an argument。

題目

allinone 學測精選練習卷 第 25 回 八、英文作文

提示：不同世代的人成長環境不同，想法與習慣也常有差異。請寫一篇英文作文，文長至少 120 個單詞，文分兩段。第一段描述一次你和家中長輩因為想法或習慣不同而發生誤會或衝突的經驗；第二段說明你認為應該如何縮小世代之間的代溝，並說明理由。

這一題的評分重點

  • 內容（5 分）：第一段具體描述一次世代間的誤會或衝突（起因、雙方反應、後來的體會），第二段提出縮小代溝的方法並說明理由。
  • 組織（5 分）：兩段分工清楚，各有主題句，用 when、so、since 等連接詞串起前後。
  • 文法句構（4 分）：時態正確（經驗用過去式、看法用現在式），能用 Only when … can …、It is worth V-ing 等句型。
  • 字彙拼字（4 分）：能用 reunion、absurd、offend、bias、the generation gap 等本回字詞，用字精確、拼字正確。
  • 格式與字數（2 分）：分兩段、段首縮排或空行，至少 120 個單詞。

大考中心怎麼給分

照 115 學測英文考科〈非選擇題滿分參考答案與評分原則〉的表一。115 年的表沒有標每級幾分，分數（5-4、3、2-1、0）取自 113、114 學測的評分原則與 115 學年度起適用的考試說明。

內容5 分

內容是否切題

優（5-4 分）主題(句)清楚切題，並有具體、完整的相關細節支持。
可（3 分）主題不夠清楚或突顯，部分相關敘述發展不全。
差（2-1 分）主題不明，大部分相關敘述發展不全或與主題無關。
劣（0 分）幾乎沒有內容或文意難辨。

組織5 分

組織是否具連貫性

優（5-4 分）重點分明，有開頭、發展、結尾，前後連貫，轉承語使用得當。
可（3 分）重點安排不妥，前後發展比例與轉承語使用欠妥。
差（2-1 分）重點不明、前後連貫性差。
劣（0 分）毫無重點、前後不連貫。

文法句構5 分

句子結構與文法

優（5-4 分）文法、格式、標點幾乎無誤，文句結構富變化。
可（3 分）文法、格式、標點偶有錯誤，但不影響文意表達。
差（2-1 分）文法、格式、標點錯誤多，明顯影響文意表達。
劣（0 分）文法、格式、標點幾乎全錯，嚴重影響文意表達。

字彙拼字5 分

用字是否適切表達文意，及拼字與標點符號使用是否正確

優（5-4 分）用字精確、得宜，拼字、大小寫幾乎無誤。
可（3 分）字詞單調、重複，用字偶有不當，拼字、大小寫偶有錯誤，但不影響文意表達。
差（2-1 分）用字、拼字、大小寫錯誤多，明顯影響文意表達。
劣（0 分）用字、拼字幾乎全錯，嚴重影響文意表達。

整體分數怎麼算

  • 英文作文依考生在內容、組織、文法句構、字彙拼字之表現評分，各項得分加總後給予一個整體分數（holistic score）。
  • 每項 5 分，合計 20 分。
  • 113、114 學測的評分原則另外寫了五等級：特優（19-20 分）、優（15-18 分）、可（10-14 分）、差（5-9 分）、劣（0-4 分）。115 年的文件沒有這一句。

扣分與 0 分

  • 字數明顯不足者，扣總分 1 分；未分段者，亦扣總分 1 分。
  • 寫多段，不扣分。字數明顯不足且未分段，不重複扣分。
  • 0 分的情況：空白、完全文不對題或離題、完全抄襲選文、文句完全錯誤或文字不足成句。

評分重點

  • 英文作文評分重點在於內容須符合題目要求，提供充分、具體之細節，句構語法及用字適切，拼字與標點符號使用得當；內容充實以及語言表達得宜，應是考生得分之重要元素。
